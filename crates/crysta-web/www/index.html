Terranigma: Crysta

A reimplementation of the first town of Terranigma, driven by the game's own scripts. It needs your own dump of the Japanese cartridge, Tenchi Souzou (Japan). The file stays on your computer: this page does not upload or store it.

Arrows or WASD: walk. X, Enter or Space: talk, confirm, lift. Z or Backspace: cancel. M: sound on or off. V: wide or classic view. A gamepad works too.
