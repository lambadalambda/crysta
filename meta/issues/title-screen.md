# Show the title screen

## Summary

The title the cartridge shows after its logos; Start opens the Restart
screen.

## Dependencies

- [Choose a game on the Restart screen](restart-file-select.md)

## Requirements

- Research the title's art, animation, music and timing on both ROMs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The title matches native frames on both ROMs, and Start opens the Restart
  screen at the native frame.
