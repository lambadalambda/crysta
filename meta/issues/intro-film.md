# Play the logos and the intro film

## Summary

The logos before the title and the intro film that plays when nobody
presses Start.

## Dependencies

- [Show the title screen](title-screen.md)

## Requirements

- Research the logos and the film on both ROMs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The logos and the film match native frames on both ROMs; any press
  leaves them for the title as natively.

## Notes

- Last of the boot work: the largest part and the least needed to play.
